## Judge Analysis

- Source: Step 5 judge (advisory; it never changes the score, a gate, the review, or a halt decision)
- Summary: Fresh verification is a fenced, fault-injecting phase between Step 4 and Step 5 that requires observed passing evidence, downgrades unproven criteria, caps rounds at three, pauses on residual defects, and always cleans its scratch worktree. The audit command compares claims to the real diff, classifies confirmed/refuted/caveats, and blocks ship according to policy, with both results recorded on run state and the delivery report.

| Severity | Evidence | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| medium | Step 4→5 transition gated by the config key. | Fresh verification is opt-in (verification.freshVerifyEnabled defaults false), so existing pipelines gain no adversarial verification until a workspace enables it. |
| low | AC5/AC6 fault semantics. | Fault plans are author-supplied strings and a no-op fault downgrades the criterion, so a weak plan yields unverified ACs rather than false confidence. |
