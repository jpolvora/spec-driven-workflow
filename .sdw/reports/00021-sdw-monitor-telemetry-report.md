## Judge Analysis

- Source: Step 5 judge (advisory; it never changes the score, a gate, the review, or a halt decision)
- Summary: Telemetry and the monitor are a read-only observability layer beside the run state: one deduplicated JSONL event per transition with bounded dispatch/gate payloads, a read-only monitor with watch/stall/JSON/run-without-state, and a report that aggregates by step and status. Append failures degrade to warnings and never change a step or run status.

| Severity | Evidence | Risk |
|---|---|---|
